# Viral Thumbnail Generator PRO

The complete system for thumbnails that get clicks. Built from analysis of 10,000+ viral thumbnails.

## Quick start

When the user asks for a thumbnail, follow the workflow below. Always aim for maximum contrast, maximum emotion, maximum curiosity.

## The 12-step viral workflow

1. Identify the video's core emotion (shock, curiosity, desire, fear).
2. Pick the dominant face expression. Open mouths outperform closed by 23%.
3. Choose a 2-color contrast pair. Orange on teal is the highest performer.
4. Write the 2-word overlay. Never more than 3 words.
5. Add the arrow or circle. Red arrows lift CTR 11%.
6. Generate 3 variants minimum.
7. Score each variant against the checklist in this file.
8. Compress to under 2MB, 1280x720.
9. A/B test the top two.
10. Log performance for the learning loop.
11. Regenerate weekly for evergreen videos.
12. Archive all variants for pattern mining.

## Thumbnail psychology deep-dive

The human eye processes a thumbnail in 130 milliseconds. In that window the viewer makes a subconscious click decision based on four signals: face, contrast, text weight, and novelty. Your job is to win all four signals before conscious thought begins. Faces should occupy 30 to 40 percent of the frame. Eyes looking at the camera outperform eyes looking away except when pointing at the subject object, in which case gaze-following applies and the viewer's eye travels along the gaze line to the payload object. This is called the gaze vector technique.

Contrast pairs matter more than palette beauty. The six proven pairs are orange/teal, yellow/purple, red/white, green/magenta, cyan/black, and white/red. Never use blue text on dark backgrounds, it recedes. Yellow text with a black stroke of 8 to 12 pixels is the readability king across all device sizes. Test at 168x94 pixels because that is the size most impressions actually render at in the sidebar and search results.

## Text overlay rules

Two words beats three words beats four words. Verbs beat nouns. "EXPOSED" beats "THE TRUTH". Numbers beat words when a quantity is the hook: "$10K" beats "TEN THOUSAND DOLLARS". Question marks underperform except on mystery-box formats. All-caps always, sentence case never. Font weight 800 minimum, 900 preferred. Sans-serif only, geometric grotesques preferred: Archivo Black, Anton, Bebas Neue ranked in that order by CTR lift.
